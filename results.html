Team · College Baseball

The Olive-Harvey turnaround

5 → 28 wins

The starting point

A community-college program coming off a five-win season — thin roster, no strength culture, players who had never trained with structure.

What we did

As assistant coach I helped install daily standards: structured strength work, position-specific development, recruiting across Chicagoland, and an expectation that preparation is non-negotiable. Strength and muscle mass increased across 100% of the roster.

The result

A school-record 28-win season — part of a climb from 5 wins to 10 to 28, with four playoff appearances and two conference championships. More lasting: young men who learned what a professional standard feels like and carried it to four-year programs. I'm still on that staff today.

The lesson: talent is common. Standards are rare. Culture is a coaching decision.
