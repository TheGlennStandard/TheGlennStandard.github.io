Questions

Frequently asked

I've never trained before. Is this for me?

Especially for you. Beginners are my favorite clients — we get to build your habits right the first time, with no bad patterns to unlearn. Your first session is an assessment and a conversation, not a test.

Where do sessions happen?

In your building's fitness center (I train residents at NEMA Chicago and other residences), at partner gyms around Chicago, or remotely. We'll pick what fits your life.

My kid plays baseball. What age do you start with?

I work with youth athletes through college players. For younger athletes, the focus is movement quality, speed, and confidence — age-appropriate, no maxing out. Parents are always welcome to watch.

I'm recovering from an injury. Can you help?

Once you're cleared by your medical provider, yes — my kinesiology background and form-first approach are built for exactly this. I'll coordinate with your physical therapist's guidance where needed.

How soon will I see results?

You'll move better within weeks. Visible change follows consistency — usually 8–12 weeks. I don't sell quick fixes; I build results that are still there in five years.

The first step is a conversation

Tell me your goals and your history. I'll tell you honestly how I can help — and if I'm not the right fit, I'll point you to someone who is.
