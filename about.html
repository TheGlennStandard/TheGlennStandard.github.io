About

The standard is personal.

I coach the way I was coached at my best — with honesty, precision, and the belief that everyone deserves a champion's preparation.

My Story

Chicago made me. Baseball raised me.

I was born and raised in Chicago, and like a lot of kids here, I found my identity on a baseball field. The game gave me structure, mentors, and a reason to take care of my body before I even understood what that meant. It carried me to Coe College, where I played my way to two conference championships and a regional title — and where I fell in love with the science behind performance, earning my degree in kinesiology.

When my playing days ended, I stayed in the dugout. I spent a season coaching at my alma mater, then joined Olive-Harvey College back home in Chicago, where I helped take a five-win program to a school-record twenty-eight wins, two conference championships, four playoff appearances, and five all-region players. Those years taught me something no textbook could: talent is common — standards are rare. The players who made it weren't always the most gifted. They were the ones who did the small things right, every single day.

I carried that lesson into performance training, spending two years at D1 Training building athletes and adults of every age, and then into the NCAA: today I'm an Assistant Strength & Conditioning Coach at Roosevelt University, supporting 17 Division II athletic programs. Alongside that, I train private clients at East Bank Club — Chicago's flagship private athletic club — and at NEMA Chicago, the largest residential gym in the Chicagoland area. And I'm still in the dugout at Olive-Harvey, where I've coached since 2021.

Whether you're a shortstop chasing a scholarship or a grandparent chasing a toddler, my job is the same: teach you to move well, build habits that last, and never let you settle for a rep you didn't earn.

That's the promise. Form first. Built to last.
