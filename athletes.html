Parents and athletes

Quick answers.

What age can an athlete start?

Early teens, with good coaching. Movement first, then load. No max testing for kids.

Will lifting make me slow?

No. Speed is force into the ground. Stronger athletes who move well run faster.

Do you work with my skills coach?

Yes. They teach the skill. I build the athlete under it, and I share throwing and swinging loads.
